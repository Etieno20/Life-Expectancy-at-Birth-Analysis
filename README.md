### Global Life Expectancy Analysis (2000–2021)

# About the Project

This project looks at life expectancy data from 196 countries between 2000 and 2021.

I used Microsoft Excel to clean, analyze, and visualize the data. I also created an interactive dashboard to show the major findings.

## Key Findings

- Global life expectancy increased from about 67 years to 73 years.
- Japan had the highest life expectancy at 84.5 years.
- Lesotho had the lowest life expectancy at 51.5 years.
- Women had a higher life expectancy than men globally.
- Africa had the lowest regional life expectancy, at about 63 years.
